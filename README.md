# JD-Xi Tutorial Hub

**Learn the Roland JD-Xi by actually using the Roland JD-Xi.**

JD-Xi Tutorial Hub is a visual, hands-on learning app for getting comfortable with the synthesizer without having to work through the manual front to back.

The JD-Xi stays at the center of every lesson. The app shows you **where to look, what to touch, what to listen for, and what to do next** — one practical step at a time.

## How to use it

**[Run JD-Xi Tutorial Hub in your browser](https://jd-xi-apps.github.io/Tutorials/)**

Or run it locally:

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the downloaded `Tutorials` folder.
3. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser.
4. Keep the app open beside your JD-Xi and work through the lessons at your own pace.

No installation, setup wizard, account, or server is required.

Firefox is the primary target, and Chromium-based browsers also work.

---

## What it includes

The core course contains **30 guided tutorials** across three levels:

- **Beginner** — learn the instrument, find sounds, use the Parts, knobs, effects, patterns, and performance controls.
- **Novice** — learn menus, sequencing, drums, bass lines, arpeggiator, sound editing, saving, and recovery.
- **Intermediate** — build your own sounds and Programs, create fuller patterns, organize your work, and perform a finished groove live.

The final sequence brings those skills together into a practical capstone: **build a groove, save it, and perform it.**

Beyond the guided course, the app also includes:

- **Hardware Explorer** — find controls on the JD-Xi and see what they do.
- **Quick Reference** — concise procedures for common tasks you may want to look up again.
- **Search** — jump directly to tutorials, steps, controls, reference procedures, and optional lessons.
- **Specialty** — optional Vocoder, AutoPitch, and Auto Note lessons.
- **Progress and Continue** — resume where you left off and track completion.
- **Bookmarked** — keep useful lessons easy to find.

## Built around the actual instrument

Tutorials use the JD-Xi itself as the visual reference, with full-panel views, highlighted controls, close-ups, and display-focused views where needed.

The course is practical and ear-first. It focuses on using the JD-Xi as a standalone instrument for sounds, patterns, and performance rather than turning the experience into a music-theory or DAW course.

The tutorial target is **JD-Xi System 1.51**.
